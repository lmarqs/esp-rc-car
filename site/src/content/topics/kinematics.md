**Forward kinematics** takes a robot arm's joint angles and works out where its tip ends up. **Inverse kinematics**
goes the other way: given where the tip should be, it finds the joint angles.

<div class="gap">

A playable example: drag the tip of a two-joint arm and watch the angles follow.
