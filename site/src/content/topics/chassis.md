The RC car is built on a 1:10 chassis you can print:

- The [DKS chassis guide](https://dukedoks.com/portfolio/guia-chasis-rc/) and its printable
  [1:10 adaptable DKS basic chassis](https://www.printables.com/model/447947-chassis-110-adaptable-dks-basic).
- An [A2212 motor mount](https://www.printables.com/model/845086-a2212-motor-mount-for-chassis-110-adaptable-dks-ba)
  for that chassis.

The car's own sketch is not written yet.
